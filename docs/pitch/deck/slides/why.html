Tenet · 03 · A failed weld means a leak, a fire or an explosion

0:33–0:53 (20 s). “Our setting: industrial radiography. Every ship hull, pipeline and pressure tank is held together by welds, and a hidden crack can mean a leak or an explosion. Radiography X-rays a weld without cutting it open. A gamma source needs no power and fits inside a tank, so it’s a standard way to prove a weld is sound.” Point at the crack in the weld, then at the dark line it leaves on the film.
