The problem statement

“Build an app that lets safety personnel keep completing security processes offline and syncs their changes correctly when internet access returns.”

Safety personnel

Professionals who protect workers, the public and property from hazards, accidents and health risks.

Security processes

Structured, repeatable steps that organisations use to manage risk and protect assets.

Offline

Working with no internet. Nearby devices can still talk directly to each other.

Syncs correctly

Every device’s changes merge into one record: nothing lost, nothing doubled, every conflict shown.
