0:53–1:13 (20 s). “The radiographer winds a sealed iridium-192 source out of a shielded camera to the weld, with film behind it and a cordon around it. Singapore’s shipyards and refineries do this every day. But the IAEA rates these sources Category 2: minutes to hours of handling can permanently injure. And if one is stolen, it’s a dirty-bomb risk.” Point at the diagram: the camera stays shielded, the source is wound out through the guide tube to the weld, and nobody may be inside the cordon.
