Actions

Button

Guidelines

The one primary action per screen (amber, cut corner, 72dp), plus secondary, danger, text and hatched-out disabled buttons.

Status

CountdownBar

Guidelines

Time left before an item is due back at the store, shown as a bar under the custody label.

HazardBand

Guidelines

The system's two 45° patterns: stripes, which mean attention, and hatching, which means not available.

OfflineBanner

Guidelines

A full-width strip under the top app bar that says whether this phone can reach the server and how many records are waiting.

SyncChip

Guidelines

A compact label showing where one record, or this phone's outbox, stands against the server.

Custody

CustodyCard

Guidelines

The answer to "who holds this item right now?" for one item, drawn as a hazard label: the hero of the home screen and the item detail screen.

ForkAlert

Guidelines

The warning label a supervisor sees when the server finds two handovers from the same giver that both follow the same record: the double handover the system can't stop offline but can prove.

HazardDiamond

Guidelines

A square turned 45° carrying the figure 7, the UN dangerous-goods class for radioactive material.

RecordPanel

Guidelines

A summary panel set like a printed form, for the supervisor's audit and export screens: the daily location record, the movements in and out of storage, today's counts.

TimelineRow

Guidelines

One record in an item's custody chain, newest at the bottom, inside a bg-100 list with 2dp line dividers and square corners.

Handover

HandoverStep

Guidelines

The parts of the handover flow: a three-step stepper, the seal check result, and the QR panel that carries an offer or an acceptance between two phones.

LiabilityLine

Guidelines

The block that marks the moment liability moves.

RadioCheck

Guidelines

The six-digit check a supervisor runs over the radio, offline, to confirm that a holder's phone has an item's latest custody record.

Receipt

Guidelines

The giver's proof that a handover happened, and the one place the system breaks its own 0°/45° rule.

Inputs

TextField

Guidelines

A square input with its label above, for the few things a person types: a seal number that won't scan, a note, a search.

Navigation

BottomNav

Guidelines

Three destinations along the bottom of the top-level screens.

TopAppBar

Guidelines

The bar at the top of every screen: where you are, a way back, and this phone's sync state.

Containers

BottomSheet

Guidelines

A panel that slides up over the current screen for a short choice: picking the receiver for a handover, choosing why a seal was replaced, confirming a report.

ListRow

Guidelines

A tappable row in a square list: people on the roster, items in the store, records in the outbox.

Feedback

EmptyState

Guidelines

What a list shows when there's nothing in it yet: what will appear, how to get it, and one action.

LoadingSkeleton

Guidelines

Placeholders while a server-only view loads (the supervisor's chain from all phones, today's records), and a segmented bar while records send.

Snackbar

Guidelines

A brief message at the bottom of the screen, above the primary action, that confirms an event or offers one follow-up.
