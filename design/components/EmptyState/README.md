# EmptyState

What a list shows when there's nothing in it yet: what will appear, how to get it, and one action.

A dashed 2dp `line-strong` box (dashed means "nothing here yet"), centred content with `space-8` padding: a 48dp Material Symbols Sharp icon for the kind of list in `ink-muted` (`inventory_2` for items, `outbox` for the outbox, `warning` for alerts, `description` for records), a title in bold uppercase mono at 20sp (NO ITEMS IN YOUR CUSTODY), one or two sentences in `body`, `ink-muted` ("Items signed over to you appear here. To receive one, scan the giver's offer."), and a secondary button (SCAN AN OFFER).

Other uses: OUTBOX EMPTY ("Every record on this phone is on the server."), NO ALERTS ("Nothing needs a decision."), NO RECORDS TODAY.

The consumer provides: the title, the sentence and an optional action.

Don't: use the Class 7 diamond (it reads as a count), an illustration, a joke or an exclamation mark, or a primary button (the empty state isn't the next step of a process).
